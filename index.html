THE POWER OF
RAMAN SPECTRA

Every molecule has a unique Raman fingerprint. We decode these spectral signatures to diagnose diseases, detect antibiotic resistance, and monitor metabolic pathways - all without labels or stains.

SYSTEM OVERVIEW
ABOUT IBPL

The central theme of IBPL is to establish and translate an integrated biophotonics approach for imaging and disease diagnosis - from morphology to biochemical dynamic equilibrium.

Our Mission

We want to make the invisible obvious. By reading the molecular fingerprints hidden inside cells and tissues, we catch the earliest signs of disease and change - clearly, gently, and without harming the sample. Better answers, sooner, for patients and the people studying them.

Our Toolkit

We point a Raman laser at a sample and listen to the light it scatters back. Add a pinch of harmless “heavy” atoms, a dash of smart software, and a microscope that maps it all - and a single cell starts telling us its life story.

RESEARCH MODULES
SHINING LIGHT ON
LIVING THINGS

Here’s the simple version: we shine harmless laser light on cells, tissues, even a drop of water, and read the faint “fingerprint” that bounces back. That fingerprint quietly tells us what something is made of and how it’s changing - no dyes, no staining, no guesswork. Here’s what we chase with it.

🧬

Proteins, caught in the act

Proteins are a cell's tiny workers. We watch them being made and switched on as it happens, so a healthy cell and a struggling one tell themselves apart.

ProteomicsLive tracking
🫧

Fats & the rhythm of metabolism

Every cell stores and burns fat in its own way. By reading those fatty signatures we follow how a cell feeds, grows and quietly changes over time.

LipidomicsMetabolism
🏷️

No dyes, no labels

Most tests stain or tag a sample first - which can change it. Our light-based read-out needs none of that, so we see things exactly as they really are.

Label-freeGentle
⚛️

Heavy-atom tracers

When we do want to follow something closely, we feed cells harmless 'heavy' atoms and watch where they travel - a tracer you can't see, but our laser can.

Isotope probingD₂O
🧪

Straight from the sample

Sometimes we carefully lift a molecule out to study it; other times we read it right inside the living cell, untouched. We do both - whichever tells the cleaner story.

In situExtraction-free
🦠

Outsmarting superbugs

Some bacteria shrug off antibiotics. We catch the warning signs fast - often within hours - so the right drug can be chosen before resistance gets ahead.

AMRColistin
🧭

Following the pathway

A cell's chemistry runs like a road map. We trace which routes light up, and when - revealing how living things are wired, one step at a time.

PathwaysSingle-cell
🌊

Hunting microplastics

Tiny plastic fragments are turning up everywhere - water, food, even us. We find them and pin down exactly which plastic each one is.

MicroplasticsEnvironment
🩺

From bench to bedside

Screening for cancer, testing for infection, checking whether a treatment is working - our tools are built to help real patients and the clinics caring for them.

DiagnosticsHealth
🏭

Out in the real world

Beyond the clinic, the same light tricks check food quality, biofuels and manufacturing - fast, clean, and without wasting a drop of the sample.
